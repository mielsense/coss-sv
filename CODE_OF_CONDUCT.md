# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Treat contributors with respect. Discuss code and evidence without attacking the person who wrote it. Welcome questions, state disagreements plainly, and give people room to correct mistakes.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing, and sustained personal attacks are not accepted in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct problems privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or ban an account when behavior puts contributors at risk or prevents useful collaboration.

Maintainers will protect the reporter's privacy where possible. Knowingly false reports and retaliation against a reporter are also violations.

This policy applies to repository discussions, reviews, issues, project chat, and public representation of the project.
